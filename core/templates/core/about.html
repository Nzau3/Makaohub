About MakaoHub

MakaoHub is a trusted rental platform designed specifically for Kenya, with a strong focus on Machakos Town and surrounding areas. We connect students, young professionals, and first-time renters with verified, quality rental properties. Our platform makes finding and listing properties simple, transparent, and secure for everyone involved.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make renting easy, safe, and fair for everyone in Kenya, especially students and first-time renters in Machakos. We believe that finding a home should be straightforward and stress-free. That's why we provide:

  • Verified Listings: Every property on MakaoHub is verified to ensure accuracy and authenticity. We work directly with landlords to confirm property details, availability, and pricing.
  • Secure Communication: Our platform enables safe, direct messaging between tenants and landlords, protecting your personal information while facilitating transparent conversations about properties.
  • Student-Friendly Approach: We understand the unique needs of students and young professionals. Our listings highlight affordable options, proximity to shopping centers, transport links, and educational institutions in Machakos.
  • Fair and Transparent Process: All rental prices are clearly displayed in Kenyan Shillings (KES), with no hidden fees. We promote honest communication and fair rental practices throughout the entire process.

Why Choose Us

MakaoHub stands out as the most reliable rental platform for Machakos and surrounding areas. Here's what makes us different:

  • Local Expertise: We specialize in Machakos Town and understand the local rental market, neighborhoods, and what matters most to renters in this area. From Machakos CBD to Mikuyuni, Matungulu, and Kangundo, we know the communities we serve.
  • Trust and Safety First: Every landlord and property is verified before listing. We prioritize your safety and peace of mind, ensuring you can browse and inquire about properties with confidence.
  • Designed for Students and First-Time Renters: Renting your first place can be overwhelming. We simplify the process with clear property descriptions, transparent pricing, and helpful resources tailored to students and young professionals starting their rental journey.
